## Product Details

### Core Functionality
- **Voice documentation** via smartphone app
- Nurses speak → AI transcribes → structures into proper medical documentation
- Integrates with existing systems (Vivendi, etc.)
- Real-time documentation at point of care

### Technical Differentiators
1. **Custom domain-specific AI** - NOT generic LLM (like ChatGPT)
   - Trained specifically on nursing processes
   - Understands medical terminology, dialects, non-native speakers
   - More explainable and precise for healthcare context

2. **Offline capability** - works without internet connection
   - Syncs when WiFi available
   - Critical for facilities with poor connectivity

3. **Noise filtering** - works in noisy environments (patient rooms, hallways)

### Hardware Requirements
- Android 10+ smartphones (~€200 each)
- 4 phones per ward recommended (shared between shifts)
- WiFi access points (at least in station room)
- No SIM cards required (WiFi-only option)

### Integrations
- Connects to existing documentation systems (Vivendi, etc.)
- Does NOT replace existing systems - supplements them

## VOIZE Weaknesses & Gaps (PflegeAI Opportunities)

### 1. **ONE-WAY ONLY** ⭐⭐⭐
- VOIZE: Nurses → Documentation
- **Gap:** No communication FROM residents TO nurses
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Bidirectional communication

### 2. **No Hardware Integration**
- VOIZE uses generic smartphones
- **Gap:** No dedicated hardware for seniors
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Smartwatch/wearable for seniors

### 3. **No Resident Interface**
- VOIZE is 100% for staff
- Residents don't interact with the system
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Resident-facing UX

### 4. **Documentation Only**
- Pure documentation tool
- No coordination/communication features
- **Gap:** Doesn't help with care requests, prioritization
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Real-time request/priority management

### 5. **Enterprise Focus Only**
- Minimum 100 beds
- No small facility solution
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Smaller facilities, pilot programs

### 6. **German Only**
- No multilingual support for documentation
- **Future opportunity:** International expansion may require this

### 7. **No Urgency/Priority System**
- Records what happened, doesn't help with what's happening NOW
- **PflegeAI opportunity:** Real-time AI triage of resident needs

## Strategic Implications for PflegeAI

### Do NOT compete head-on:
VOIZE has:
- $50M funding
- YC backing
- 1,100 customers
- Established brand
- Charité study validation

### Instead: Complement & Differentiate

**Position PflegeAI as:**
> "VOIZE handles nurse documentation. We handle resident communication."

**The "1+1=3" pitch:**
- VOIZE saves 30% documentation time
- PflegeAI saves response time, improves care quality
- Together: Complete care digitalization

### Key differentiators to emphasize:

1. **Bidirectional** - We connect residents and nurses
2. **Hardware play** - Smartwatch = patent-worthy, sticky
3. **Real-time prioritization** - AI triage of needs
4. **Resident experience** - Seniors have a voice
5. **Integration partner** - Can work WITH VOIZE, not against
