## Executive Summary

PflegeAI's unit economics are favorable for a B2B SaaS play in healthcare:
- **CAC:** €2,500-4,000 (blended) — higher than avg SaaS but justified by high LTV
- **LTV:** €35,000-50,000 per facility — driven by low churn and expansion revenue
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 10-15x — exceptional, above 3x benchmark
- **Payback Period:** 6-9 months — better than 18-month SaaS median
- **Annual Churn:** 5-8% target — sticky B2B with high switching costs

**Key Insight:** Healthcare SaaS has elevated churn industry-wide (7.5% monthly!) due to 2024-2025 budget pressures. PflegeAI can outperform by creating hardware lock-in and deep workflow integration.

## 3. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

### LTV Formula

```
LTV = (ARPA × Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn Rate
```

Where:
- ARPA = Average Revenue Per Account (monthly)
- Gross Margin = 70-75% for SaaS (target)
- Monthly Churn Rate = 0.5% (target)

### PflegeAI LTV Calculation

**Assumptions (50-bed facility, 30 smartwatches):**
| Revenue Component | Monthly | Annual |
|-------------------|---------|--------|
| Documentation Module | €299 | €3,588 |
| Communication (€8 × 50 beds) | €400 | €4,800 |
| Hardware HaaS (€12 × 30 devices) | €360 | €4,320 |
| **Total ARPA** | **€1,059** | **€12,708** |

**LTV Scenarios:**

| Churn Rate | Customer Lifetime | LTV (75% margin) |
|------------|------------------|------------------|
| 1% monthly (12% annual) | 8.3 years | €79,200 |
| 0.67% monthly (8% annual) | 12.4 years | €118,260 |
| 0.5% monthly (6% annual) | 16.7 years | €158,925 |

**Conservative LTV Estimate:** €40,000-50,000
- Assumes 4-5 year average customer lifetime
- Accounts for some churn above target
- Doesn't include expansion revenue

### Expansion Revenue Opportunity

| Expansion Path | Potential Uplift |
|----------------|------------------|
| Add more smartwatches | +€12/device/month |
| Roll out to additional facilities (chains) | +100% per facility |
| Premium AI features | +€100-200/month |
| Analytics/reporting add-on | +€99/month |

**Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Target:** 110-120%
- Best-in-class B2B SaaS: >120%
- Median: 106%
- Enterprise-focused: 115-125%

## 5. CAC Payback Period

### Industry Benchmarks

| Segment | Average Payback | Good Payback |
|---------|-----------------|--------------|
| SMB SaaS | 9 months | 6 months |
| Mid-Market | 14-18 months | 9-12 months |
| Enterprise | 24 months | 18 months |
| Medtech SaaS | 11-14 months | 9-11 months |

**Source:** FirstPageSage 2025, BVP, KeyBanc

### PflegeAI Payback Calculation

```
Payback = CAC / (Monthly ARPA × Gross Margin)
Payback = €3,000 / (€1,059 × 0.75)
Payback = €3,000 / €794
Payback = 3.8 months
```

**Even with €4,000 CAC:**
```
Payback = €4,000 / €794 = 5.0 months
```

**Assessment:** PflegeAI's payback period of 4-6 months is exceptional:
- Better than Medtech average (11-14 months)
- Better than mid-market average (14-18 months)
- Driven by high ARPA from hybrid pricing model
