## Market Trends (Pitch Points)

### 1. Growing Demand
- German population 65+: 22% → 30% by 2060
- Care recipients projected to hit 6.5M+ by 2030
- Chronic nursing shortage → tech becomes essential

### 2. Digitalization Push
- Government "Krankenhauszukunftsgesetz" funding digital health
- COVID accelerated digital adoption in care facilities
- Younger facility managers more tech-forward

### 3. Consolidation
- Small operators closing (-0.9%/year)
- Private equity entering (larger facilities, more tech budget)
- Chain operators need scalable solutions

### 4. Pricing Power
- Care costs rising (€2,500-4,500/month per resident)
- Software is <0.5% of operating costs → easy to justify
- ROI clear: save 30min/nurse/day = massive savings
