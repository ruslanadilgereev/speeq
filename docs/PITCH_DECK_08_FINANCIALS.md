## Objection Handling

### "These projections are aggressive"

> "Fair point. Let me show you our conservative case: €2.5M ARR by Year 3, still with 8x LTV:CAC. Even in the downside, we build a profitable healthcare SaaS business. The upside comes from three things VOIZE can't do: bidirectional communication, hardware revenue, and premium pricing for our blue ocean."

### "How do you get to €1,000 ARPA?"

> "Three stacked revenue streams:
> 1. €299/month for documentation (competitive with VOIZE)  
> 2. €400/month for resident communication (€8 × 50 beds) — no competitor offers this
> 3. €360/month for smartwatch hardware-as-a-service (€12 × 30 devices)
> 
> This is a 50-bed facility with 60% device penetration. Larger facilities = larger deals."

### "Why is your churn so low?"

> "Hardware lock-in. When you have 30 smartwatches deployed across a facility with trained residents and staff, switching means replacing physical devices, retraining everyone, and losing historical data. That's a 6-month project. VOIZE is pure software — a facility can switch in weeks. That's the structural difference."

### "What about healthcare budget pressures?"

> "Healthcare SaaS churn did spike in 2024-2025 — 67% increase. But we've built in three mitigations: multi-year contracts, clear ROI proof (4 min response time vs 15 min), and §113 SGB XI — German law explicitly says facilities keep efficiency savings from digital tools. Our software pays for itself."
