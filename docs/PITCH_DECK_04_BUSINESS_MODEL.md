## Common Investor Questions & Answers

**Q: "Why would someone pay €8/resident when the call button is free?"**
> A: The call button is binary and context-free. Our system captures *what* they need, *how urgent* it is, and routes to the right nurse. One prevented fall pays for a year of service. One avoided hospital transfer saves €5,000+.

**Q: "Isn't hardware a distraction for a SaaS company?"**
> A: Hardware-as-a-Service is our moat. VOIZE is pure software — customers can switch in a month. Our smartwatches create physical switching costs and recurring revenue. Every month a customer pays, the hardware is more amortized and margins improve.

**Q: "How do you compete with VOIZE on price?"**
> A: We don't compete on documentation price — we match it. We compete on *value*. VOIZE can't do resident communication. That's our blue ocean. Same documentation at same price, plus €400/month for features they don't have.

**Q: "What's the gross margin on hardware?"**
> A: After 12 months, hardware is fully amortized and margins exceed 85%. Year 1 blended margin is ~70%, Year 2+ is 75%+. The HaaS model actually *improves* margins over time.

**Q: "Why would a chain sign an enterprise deal?"**
> A: One pilot facility with proven ROI → rollout to 20-50 locations. That's €200-500K ACV. The top 30 operators control 23% of the market. One enterprise sale = 50 individual sales.
