## Pitch Delivery Notes

### The Story Arc
1. **Start concrete:** "11,646 nursing homes in Germany. 914,000 beds. 1.2 million workers."
2. **Show the money:** "At €600/month average, that's a €100 million market. Germany alone."
3. **Prove it's fundable:** "VOIZE just raised $50M at 1,100 facilities. Investors are betting on this space."
4. **Show your slice:** "We're targeting €4.2 million ARR by Year 3 — that's 360 facilities, or 3% of the reachable market."
5. **Expand the vision:** "Then Europe. 70,000 facilities. €500 million."

### Key Stats to Memorize
- **11,646** — nursing homes in Germany
- **€105M** — TAM Germany
- **€500M** — TAM Europe
- **$50M** — VOIZE Series A (market validation)
- **€4.2M** — Our Year 3 ARR target

### Objection Handling

**"Isn't €100M too small for a VC-scale business?"**
> "Germany is the beachhead. Europe is €500M. And we're not just capturing market — we're creating new revenue with resident communication that no one else does. This is a blue ocean within an existing market."

**"How did you calculate TAM?"**
> "11,646 facilities times €750/month average revenue per facility. Conservative — VOIZE-style documentation alone is €300/month. We add €450+ with resident communication and hardware."

**"Can you really get to 360 customers in 3 years?"**
> "VOIZE reached 1,100 facilities in similar timeframe. The playbook is proven: pilot with one facility → prove ROI → roll out to chain. Top 30 operators control 23% of the market. One chain deal can be 20-50 facilities."

**"Why start with Germany?"**
> "Largest elderly care market in EU. German language and regulatory complexity create moat. Once certified in Germany (toughest requirements), expansion to DACH and EU is faster. Also: VOIZE proved the German market buys."
