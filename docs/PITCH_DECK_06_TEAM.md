## Why the Team Slide Matters

### Investor Reality Check
At pre-seed/seed stage, **investors bet on the jockey, not the horse.**

From Josh Kopelman (First Round Capital):
> "We invest at the earliest stage, so everything we see is wrong. The product is wrong. The pricing is wrong. The go-to-market is wrong. So fundamentally, we have to bet on the founders."

### The Data
- Team slide = **2nd most-viewed** slide in pitch decks (DocSend research)
- Investors spend **15% of total deck time** on Team slide
- Gender-diverse teams raise **57% more funding** overall

---

## What Investors Look For (Ranked by Importance)

### 1. Passion & Commitment (47%)
- Why are YOU the ones solving this problem?
- Evidence of resilience, grit, perseverance
- Personal connection to the problem

### 2. Strong Team Dynamic (31%)
- How do you know each other? How long?
- Complementary skills (technical, business, domain)
- History of working together successfully

### 3. Unique Skills or Access (31%)
- Special access to data, customers, technology
- Industry connections (nursing home operators, associations)
- Technical expertise competitors lack (AI, healthcare, hardware)

## The "Why Us" Narrative (CRITICAL)

### Framework: Founder-Market Fit
Investors want to know: **Why is THIS team uniquely positioned to solve THIS problem?**

Answer ONE of these:
1. **Lived experience:** "My grandmother is in a nursing home. I saw nurses spend more time on paperwork than with her."
2. **Domain expertise:** "Combined 15 years in healthcare IT. We've built and deployed clinical systems before."
3. **Technical edge:** "We built voice AI systems at [Company]. We know how to ship reliable STT in noisy environments."
4. **Unfair advantage:** "Our pilot partner's medical director is an advisor. We have insider access to 20+ nursing homes."

### PflegeAI "Why Us" Options

**Option A (Execution Evidence):**
> "We went from idea to pilot partner in 30 days. That's our superpower — shipping fast in healthcare."

**Option B (Domain + Technical):**
> "Healthcare experience meets AI expertise. We've built systems that nurses actually use."

**Option C (Personal Connection):**
> "This isn't just a startup. [Founder] watched their grandmother struggle to communicate needs in a nursing home. We're building what we wished existed."

## What NOT to Include

| Don't | Why |
|-------|-----|
| More than 5 people | Overwhelms the slide, dilutes focus |
| Non-founding employees | Investors fund founders, not early hires |
| Irrelevant credentials | "Won cooking competition" ≠ healthcare AI |
| Full paragraph bios | Keep to 2-3 bullets max |
| Advisors who won't help | If they won't take calls, don't list them |
| Stock photos | Always use real photos |

## Visual Design Guidelines

### Do's
- High-quality, professional photos (same style/lighting)
- Clean white or light background
- Names in bold, roles in lighter text
- 2-3 bullet points per person max
- Left-to-right reading order (CEO first)

### Don'ts
- Cartoon avatars or AI-generated faces
- Mismatched photo styles
- Walls of text
- More than 5 people on slide
- Crowded layout

## Slide Positioning in Deck

### Where to Place Team Slide

| Position | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Early (Slide 3-4)** | "Bet on founders" narrative | Delays problem/solution |
| **Middle (Slide 6-7)** | After traction, before ask | Standard, expected |
| **Late (Slide 9-10)** | Problem/solution/market first | May feel like afterthought |

**Recommendation for PflegeAI:** Place after Traction (Slide 6) 

Story arc: Problem → Solution → Market → Business Model → Traction → **Team** → Competition → Financials → Ask

This positions Team as: "Now you've seen our traction. Here's why we can 10x it."

---

## Competitive Team Positioning

### vs VOIZE (1,100 facilities, $50M raised)
Don't say: "We're better than VOIZE's team"
Do say: "VOIZE optimized for nurse documentation. We're building for resident communication — a different problem requiring different expertise."

### vs Big Tech (Google, Microsoft)
Don't say: "We're smarter than Google"
Do say: "Healthcare requires domain expertise and regulatory knowledge that big tech doesn't prioritize. We're purpose-built for German nursing homes."

## Summary: The Perfect Team Slide

1. **3-4 founders max** with photos
2. **2-3 bullets each** (relevant credentials only)
3. **One "Why Us" statement** (founder-market fit)
4. **Optional:** 1-2 notable advisors
5. **Design:** Clean, visual, scannable in 10 seconds

**The investor should think:** "These people understand the problem, have the skills to build the solution, and have proven they can execute together."
