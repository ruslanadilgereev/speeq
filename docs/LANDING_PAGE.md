# PflegeAI Landing Page Strategy

## Purpose
Convert nursing home decision-makers (Heimleitung, PDL, IT) into pilot program leads. Primary goal: collect email for demo/pilot request.

---

## Key Learnings (B2B SaaS Best Practices 2025)

1. **Problem-first hero:** Lead with the pain, not features
2. **5 or fewer form fields:** +120% conversion vs longer forms
3. **Social proof throughout:** Logos, testimonials, stats — not just one section
4. **Mobile-first:** 68%+ of B2B buyers research on mobile
5. **Video for complex products:** Keeps visitors on page, explains visually
6. **Personalization:** Industry-specific messaging converts better

---

## Target Audience

| Persona | Pain Points | What They Need to See |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Heimleitung/GF** | Staff costs, liability, family complaints | ROI, risk reduction, competitive advantage |
| **PDL (Pflegedienstleitung)** | Documentation burden, response times, staff burnout | Time savings, workflow integration, ease of use |
| **IT-Leiter** | DSGVO compliance, integration complexity | Security certifications, German hosting, API compatibility |

## Design Guidelines

### Colors
- **Primary:** #2563EB (Trust Blue)
- **Secondary:** #10B981 (Action Green)
- **Accent:** #F59E0B (Urgency Orange/Yellow)
- **Background:** #F9FAFB (Light Gray)
- **Text:** #111827 (Near Black)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, large (48px hero, 32px sections)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Source Sans)
- **German-appropriate:** Professional, not too playful

### Imagery
- Real photos (not stock if possible) of:
  - Elderly person with smartwatch
  - Nurse looking at phone notification
  - Happy care interaction
- Avoid: Clinical/hospital feel, sad elderly
- Goal: Warm, professional, trustworthy

### Mobile Optimization
- Single column layout
- Touch-friendly CTAs (min 44x44px)
- Sticky CTA button on mobile
- Compress images aggressively
