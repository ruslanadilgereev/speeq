# Pitch Deck Slide 5: Traction

## Context for Early-Stage Startups

PflegeAI is pre-revenue and pre-product. For such early stages, investors look for **validation signals** rather than revenue metrics:
- Competition wins = market validation
- Committed pilot partners = demand proof
- Team formation = execution capability
- Research depth = market understanding

The goal: Show momentum and reduce perceived risk.

## Handling the "But You Have No Revenue" Question

**Investor objection:** "This is very early. Why should I believe you can execute?"

**Response framework:**

1. **Acknowledge:** "You're right — we're pre-revenue. But we believe we're de-risked beyond most pre-revenue startups."

2. **Evidence stack:**
   - "Competition win validates the idea resonates with healthcare experts"
   - "Pilot partner proves demand exists and facilities will trial new solutions"
   - "VOIZE's $50M raise proves VCs see this market as fundable"
   - "Our research depth shows we understand the landscape better than most seed-stage teams"

3. **Ask reframe:** "We're not asking you to bet on a concept. We're asking you to fund the MVP that turns our pilot into paying customers."

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Lead with the strongest validation (hackathon win + pilot partner)
- Use a quote from pilot partner if available
- Show timeline/momentum (things are moving)
- Be specific: "5 residents" > "pilot planned"
- Connect traction to market size (11,600 facilities = huge opportunity)

### ❌ Don't
- Apologize for being early ("We're just getting started...")
- Use vague language ("We've had interest from several facilities")
- Show vanity metrics (social media followers, website visits)
- Overstate: "LOI" ≠ "contract", "interested" ≠ "committed"
- Crowd the slide — 3-4 points maximum

## Comparables: How Other Pre-Revenue Startups Framed Traction

### Airbnb (Pre-revenue)
- "First weekend: 3 guests, $80 revenue"
- *Lesson: Tiny numbers are fine if they prove the concept works*

### Dropbox (Pre-product)
- "70,000 signups from waitlist video"
- *Lesson: Demand signals (waitlist, LOIs) can substitute for revenue*

### Buffer (Early stage)
- "Launched MVP in 7 days, 100 users in first week"
- *Lesson: Speed of execution is itself traction*

### PflegeAI Framing
- "30 days from hackathon to pilot partner"
- "1 facility committed, 5 residents ready"
- "11,600 facilities mapped, €100M TAM validated"
