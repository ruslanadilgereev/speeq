# PflegeAI Pitch Deck: Problem Slide

## The Slide Content (for presentation)

### Headline
**"Nursing Homes Are Drowning in Paperwork While Residents Can't Be Heard"**

---

### The Three-Part Problem

#### 1. 🔇 Residents Can't Communicate Effectively
- Elderly residents with dementia, cognitive decline, or mobility issues struggle to use traditional call buttons
- **Average response time: 10-15 minutes** (regulation expects 3 minutes)
- In understaffed facilities: **45+ minutes** to answer call lights
- **Result:** 50% of nursing home residents fall annually; 10-25% require hospitalization

#### 2. 📋 Nurses Drown in Documentation
- Nurses spend **40% of every shift on documentation** instead of patient care
- **Only 25% of time** goes to direct patient contact
- **92% of nurses** say EHR systems hurt their job satisfaction (2025 survey)
- Time spent on paperwork has **increased 128%** since paper-based systems

#### 3. 👩‍⚕️ The Staffing Crisis Makes Everything Worse
- Germany has **35,000+ vacant nursing positions** (2025)
- Nursing shortage correlates with decreased nurse-to-resident ratios
- Health Minister Lauterbach: *"There will be a serious problem in ensuring long-term care"*
- Small operators are closing (-0.9% facilities YoY)

---

### The Cost of the Status Quo

| Problem | Human Cost | Financial Cost |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| Slow response times | Falls, injuries, deaths | €10K+ per fall hospitalization |
| Documentation burden | Burnout, turnover, errors | €50K+ to replace one nurse |
| Staffing crisis | Lower care quality | Facilities closing, families scrambling |

## Pitch Delivery Notes

### The Story Arc
1. **Start with emotion:** "Imagine being 85, confused, in pain, pressing a call button... and waiting 15 minutes."
2. **Pivot to scale:** "This happens millions of times a year in Germany's 11,600 nursing homes."
3. **Explain root cause:** "Nurses aren't ignoring residents—they're trapped doing paperwork. 40% of every shift."
4. **Connect to crisis:** "And there aren't enough nurses anyway. 35,000 positions are unfilled."
5. **Land the punch:** "The system is broken. Residents can't communicate. Nurses can't care. Something has to change."

### Key Stats to Memorize
- **40%** — time nurses spend on documentation
- **10-15 min** — average call response time
- **50%** — residents who fall each year
- **35,000** — vacant nursing positions in Germany
- **92%** — nurses who say EHR hurts their job

### Objection Handling
**"Isn't this just about adding more staff?"**
> "More staff would help, but they don't exist. Germany has 35,000 unfilled positions. Technology must multiply the impact of existing nurses, not wait for imaginary ones."

**"Call buttons already exist"**
> "Call buttons are binary: on or off. They can't express 'I'm in pain' vs 'I want water' vs 'I fell.' Nurses can't prioritize without information. Our AI captures context, summarizes, and routes intelligently."
