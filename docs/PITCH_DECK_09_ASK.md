## Objection Handling

### "Why raise now vs. after MVP?"
> "Our pilot partner is ready now. Raising lets us build the MVP properly (3 months) rather than rushing it on a shoestring. The best time to raise is when you have demand but need capital to fulfill it — that's exactly where we are."

### "€500K seems high for pre-seed"
> "It's actually standard for German B2B SaaS pre-seed rounds in 2025-2026. We benchmarked against 20+ recent raises. €500K gives us 18 months of runway — the minimum needed to prove PMF and set up a clean seed round. Raising less means rushing to raise again in 9 months while trying to build."

### "What if the pilot doesn't convert?"
> "We're building with 5 facilities, not 1. If our first pilot doesn't convert, we'll have data on why and 4 more to iterate with. The €50K hardware budget covers 150+ devices across multiple facilities — we're not betting on a single customer."

### "Why should we invest if VOIZE is winning?"
> "VOIZE is winning nurse documentation — and they've validated the market for us. But they don't do resident communication. They're one-way, we're bidirectional. They use phones, we use smartwatches. They have no real-time push. Our positioning is 'VOIZE gave nurses their voice. PflegeAI gives residents theirs.' That's a €250M+ blue ocean inside a €500M market."

### "What's your backup plan?"
> "If we can't raise €500K, we can bootstrap to MVP with €150K and a smaller pilot. It extends timeline by 6-9 months and limits us to 5 facilities instead of 20, but we can still prove the core thesis. We'd prefer to do this right with proper funding."

## Key Principles Applied

### 1. Be Specific
- Exact amount: €500K
- Exact allocation: percentages that add to 100%
- Exact milestones: dates and numbers

### 2. Show the Path
- Pre-Seed → Seed → A → B trajectory
- Each round unlocks next stage
- Investors see their exit path

### 3. Create Urgency
- "Closing Q2 2026"
- Trade show timing (April 2026)
- Pilot partner waiting for MVP
- Window before competitors move

### 4. Make It Easy to Say Yes
- Standard terms (SAFE, reasonable cap)
- Clear use of funds
- De-risked by pilot partner
- Proven market (VOIZE validation)
