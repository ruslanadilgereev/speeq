# Video Demo: Smartwatch-Integration

## Overview

**Purpose:** 60-90 second demo video showing the PflegeAI smartwatch workflow for investor pitches, landing page, and sales conversations.

**Key Message:** "In under 8 seconds, a resident's voice request reaches the right caregiver with AI-prioritized urgency."

**Format:** Screen recordings + mockups + optional voiceover

---

## Demo Video Strategy

### Why Video Matters
- YC startups with deck videos see **1.7× higher intro-to-meeting conversion** (Flowjam survey)
- Average VC spends only **2 min 23 sec** on a pitch deck — video captures attention
- Async fundraising: Share link before calls, let video work while you sleep

## Storyboard: 60-Second Pitch Demo

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:08)
**Visual:** Stock footage or illustration of elderly person in care home, looking at analog call button
**Text Overlay:** "Today, residents can only press a button. No voice. No context."
**Audio:** Soft, slightly melancholic music

---

### Scene 2: The Hardware (0:08 - 0:15)
**Visual:** PflegeAI smartwatch mockup on elderly wrist (use Mediamodifier or Freepik mockups)
**Text Overlay:** "PflegeAI gives them their voice back."
**Transition:** Watch screen lights up, microphone icon appears

---

### Scene 3: Resident Makes Request (0:15 - 0:25)
**Visual:** Smartwatch UI mockup showing:
1. Resident taps watch face
2. "Sprechen Sie jetzt..." appears with voice wave animation
3. Speech bubble shows: *"Mir ist schwindelig, ich glaube ich bin hingefallen"*

**Text Overlay:** "Frau Schmidt, 85, speaks her need in her own words."
**Audio:** German voice recording (elderly female voice, can be synthesized with ElevenLabs)

---

### Scene 4: AI Processing (0:25 - 0:35)
**Visual:** Animated flowchart or simple motion graphics:
```
🎤 Voice → 🧠 AI Transcription → ⚡ Priority Analysis → 📲 Push Notification
```

**Text Overlays (appear sequentially):**
- "AI transcribes in 2 seconds"
- "Priority: 9/10 — FALL DETECTED"
- "Auto-escalation if no response in 60s"

**Audio:** Tech-forward sound effect, slight urgency

---

### Scene 5: Caregiver Gets Notified (0:35 - 0:45)
**Visual:** Split screen:
- Left: Smartphone notification appears (lockscreen mockup)
- Right: Dashboard view from `/requests` page showing new red priority request

**Notification Preview:**
```
🔴 NOTFALL | Zimmer 214
Frau Schmidt: "Mir ist schwindelig, ich bin hingefallen"
[ÜBERNEHMEN]
```

**Text Overlay:** "Pfleger Jonas gets alerted instantly — with full context."
**Audio:** Notification sound effect

---

### Scene 6: Response + Resolution (0:45 - 0:55)
**Visual:** 
1. Caregiver taps "Übernehmen" button
2. Dashboard shows request moved to "In Bearbeitung"
3. Timer shows: "Response time: 4 minutes"

**Text Overlay:** "From voice to response in under 5 minutes. Not 15."
**Audio:** Uplifting resolution tone

---

### Scene 7: Call to Action (0:55 - 1:00)
**Visual:** PflegeAI logo + tagline
**Text Overlay:** 
- "VOIZE gave nurses their voice."
- "PflegeAI gives residents theirs."
- www.pflegeai.de | Pilotprogramm jetzt starten

**Audio:** Music crescendo, fade out

- [ ] Color grade for consistency
- [ ] Export: 1080p MP4, 16:9 ratio

- [ ] Embed in pitch deck (Google Slides or Keynote)

- [ ] Upload to data room (Notion, DocSend)
